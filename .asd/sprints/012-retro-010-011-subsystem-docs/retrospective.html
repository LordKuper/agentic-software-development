4 entries · 4 actions · 4 systemic proposals · updated 2026-09-14

Outcome

4 entries analysed, 0 consumer actions, 4 framework actions, 4 systemic proposals.

The sprint delivered everything in scope, AC-1..AC-19:

  • seven open retro-010/011 actions and seven selected proposals;
  • the subsystem registry move to docs/architecture/subsystems.md, with per-subsystem files;
  • a mermaid diagram inline in the registry.

Seven plan tasks ran in five waves. Impl-review took five iterations with 12 → 6 → 3 → 2 → 0 counted findings (floor low → critical), and the terminal full suite was green at 197/197. The registry and the tool-policy refusal rule were largely settled in iteration 1. Most of the review cost came from one criterion, AC-13, the sprint-mediated settings change:

  • iteration 1 found placement and ordering gaps;
  • iteration 2 caught the orchestrator reversing its own earlier disposition on key validation;
  • iterations 3 and 4 followed the consequences of that fix into the blocker list, the template markers and a test assertion.

Two of the four friction entries are orchestrator errors, and one of them repeated the 010 F-5 pattern that this sprint remediated: payloads that told agents to act outside their tool policy.
